# Errata and corrections

Found a listing that does not run as printed, or a tool change that broke one? Open an issue.
Corrections are triaged by the book's maintenance owner by class (blocker, major, minor, cosmetic)
and posted here and on the book's page at vertexpress.io. The scheduled CI run is the first line of
defense: when a tool release breaks a listing, the monthly job goes red and the owner is notified
before most readers notice.
